Mothertheresa666 is a multimedia project that blends storytelling, absurdity, collage, digital media, and personal narrative.

Based in Rochester, NY, Theresa is a collage artist and social media specialist. She uses her work, both analog and digital, to explore her relationship with religion, spirituality, queerness, and community.
